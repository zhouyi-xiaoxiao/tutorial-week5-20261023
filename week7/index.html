Introduction to Pure Mathematics

Thu 5 Nov · 09:00–10:00
PRIORY RD CMPLX F BLK 3F11

Key points

  • Image and pre-image are subsets of different spaces, so always name the domain and codomain.
  • Injective means equal outputs force equal inputs. Surjective means every codomain element is hit. Bijective means both.
  • Use definitions for abstract proofs and verify every counterexample.

Questions

  1. Classify 2x−3, x² with codomain ℝ, and x² with codomain [0,∞).
  2. For p(x)=x²−1, find the image of [−1,2] and the pre-image of [0,3].
  3. Prove that a composition of injective maps is injective, then give a surjective non-injective example.
  4. Use two PS5 peer submissions to identify one clear step and one step needing a stronger definition or justification.

Linear Algebra

Fri 6 Nov · 09:00–10:00
FRY BLDG G.14

Key points

  • Span, linear independence, basis, and dimension are linked: a basis is independent and spanning.
  • For subspaces, dim(X+Y)=dim X+dim Y−dim(X∩Y).
  • Row reduction reveals pivots, rank, free variables, and the null space.
  • Rank–nullity: dim ker T + dim im T = dim(domain).

Questions

  1. Show that {(x,y,z,x+iy)} ⊂ ℂ⁴ is a subspace. Find a basis and its dimension.
  2. Test whether dim(X+Y)=m+k always holds, then state the correct formula and give examples.
  3. Test the independence of (1,2,−1),(1,1,1),(0,1,−2).
  4. Find the null space and rank of [[1,2,1],[2,4,2],[1,1,0]].
  5. For T(x,y,z)=(x+y,y+z), find kernel, image, rank, and nullity.

Quick self-check

  • Have I written the domain and codomain for every function?
  • Can I explain image versus pre-image?
  • Can I show both spanning and independence for a basis?
  • Can I read pivots and free variables after row reduction?
  • Can I check the rank–nullity equation?
