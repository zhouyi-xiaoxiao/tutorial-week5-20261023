Teaching route

Each tutorial follows the same rhythm so students know what to do next.

Pure Mathematics

1
RecallElement order, cyclic groups and the subgroup theorem.
2
Cyclic subgroupsUse orders in a cyclic group, then list the cyclic subgroups of D₁₂.
3
HomomorphismsClassify maps and connect kernel and image to injectivity and surjectivity.
4
Isomorphism and exit ticketSwap factors in a direct product and distinguish groups by element orders.
Reminder Problem Sheet 7 and Quiz 7 are due at 13:00 on Thursday 19 November. Give method prompts only in the room.

Linear Algebra

1
RecallOrdered bases, coordinate vectors and matrices of linear maps.
2
Change of basisCompute Cᴱₐ and Cᴱᵦ, then reverse the direction with the inverse.
3
Matrix of a mapRead Mᴮₐ(f) as a three-stage coordinate route and construct it from basis images.
4
Checks and exit ticketUse identity products and one-basis-vector tests to catch direction errors.
Reminder Keep the notation direction visible. The rightmost matrix acts first.
