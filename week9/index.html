What students should leave with

  1. Recognise a cyclic subgroup and calculate with a generator.
  2. Test a proposed homomorphism using the operation.
  3. Use kernel/image language to distinguish injective and surjective maps.
  4. See a direct product as a group built componentwise.
Recall · 09:00–09:05 · no notes

Three quick questions

Give students two minutes alone, then check together.

1 · Order of an element

What is the order of a group element? State it in one sentence.

Board answerThe least positive integer n with xⁿ = e, if such an n exists. Otherwise the order is infinite.

2 · Cyclic group

What is a cyclic group? Use generator notation.

Board answerA group G is cyclic if G = ⟨g⟩ for some g ∈ G.

3 · Subgroup theorem

True or false: every subgroup of a cyclic group is cyclic. Commit before discussion.

Board answerTrue. This is the theorem we will use in Round 1.
Round 1 · 09:05–09:20 · 15 min

Cyclic subgroups and element orders

WATCH → TRY → COMPARE · Use PS8 A1 and A2 as the question bank.

Worked example · orders in a cyclic group

Let G = ⟨x⟩ with |x| = 30. Find |x⁶|, |x¹²| and |x²⁵|.

Board answerUse |xⁱ| = 30 / gcd(30, i). Therefore |x⁶| = 5, |x¹²| = 5 and |x²⁵| = 6.

Your turn · cyclic subgroups of D₁₂

Write D₁₂ = {e, a, …, a⁵, b, ab, …, a⁵b}. Find all distinct cyclic subgroups.

Board answer⟨e⟩, ⟨a⟩ = ⟨a⁵⟩, ⟨a²⟩ = ⟨a⁴⟩, ⟨a³⟩, and the six order-two subgroups ⟨aⁱb⟩ for i = 0,…,5. There is one subgroup of order 3 and one of order 6.
Say “The generator is a choice of element. Different generators can name the same subgroup. Count subgroups by the sets they contain, not by the labels we write.”
先让学生写出几个 ⟨aⁱ⟩，再问哪些重复。反射元素都阶为 2，因此各自产生一个二元子群。
Fast check Why does adding a reflection to a rotation subgroup not produce a cyclic subgroup containing both?
Board answer · A cyclic subgroup containing a reflection and a nontrivial rotation would need one element whose powers include both behaviours. In D₁₂ the reflection has order 2 and the rotation subgroup is distinct. Use the listed subgroups to make the obstruction concrete.
Round 2 · 09:20–09:35 · 15 min

Homomorphisms, kernels and images

WATCH → TRY → COMPARE · Connect to PS8 B1 and C4.

Worked example · a homomorphism into matrices

For z = x + yi ∈ C\{0}, define φ(z) = [[x, −y], [y, x]]. Explain why φ(zw) = φ(z)φ(w).

Board answerMultiply the two 2×2 matrices and compare with (x + yi)(s + ti) = (xs − yt) + (ys + xt)i. The entries match. The determinant x² + y² is nonzero, so the image lies in GL₂(R).

Your turn · classify three maps

For each map, decide homomorphism, injective and surjective. (a) φ: (Z,+) → (2Z,+), φ(n) = 2n. (b) ψ: (Z,+) → (Z,+), ψ(n) = n + 1. (c) θ: (Z,+) → ({1,−1},×), θ(n) = (−1)ⁿ.

Board answer(a) Homomorphism, injective and surjective. (b) Not a homomorphism because ψ(0+0)=1 but ψ(0)+ψ(0)=2. It is bijective as a function. (c) Homomorphism and surjective, not injective because θ(0)=θ(2).
Say “A homomorphism respects the operation. Injectivity and surjectivity are separate questions about fibres and the target.”
把“保持运算”“单射”“满射”分开检查。先代入运算，再问核和像。
Kernel/image bridge For a homomorphism φ:G→H, define ker φ={g:φ(g)=eH} and im φ={φ(g):g∈G}. Which property does a trivial kernel force?
Board answer · A trivial kernel forces injectivity. Surjectivity is the statement im φ = H. Do not conflate the two.
Round 3 + exit · 09:35–09:55 · optional if time

Isomorphism and direct products

Use PS8 A4, A6 and B3 as follow-up.

Worked example · swapping factors

Show H×K ≅ K×H using φ(h,k) = (k,h).

Board answerThe inverse is ψ(k,h) = (h,k). Also φ((h₁,k₁)(h₂,k₂)) = (k₁k₂,h₁h₂) = φ(h₁,k₁)φ(h₂,k₂).

Your turn · distinguish groups of order 8

Explain why C₂×C₂×C₂, C₄×C₂ and C₈ are pairwise non-isomorphic.

Board answerThe maximum element orders are 2, 4 and 8 respectively. An isomorphism preserves element orders, so no pair can be isomorphic.

Exit ticket

  1. Give one reason a subgroup can have several generators.
  2. What must you check to prove a map is a homomorphism?
  3. What does ker φ = {e} tell you?
Answers · Generators can produce the same cyclic subgroup. Check φ(xy)=φ(x)φ(y) for all x,y. A trivial kernel implies injectivity.
At home Finish PS8 A1–A4 and read the assigned question. For the assessed PS7 question, submit a complete proof before 13:00 today.

Cut rule
Rounds 1 and 2 are the lesson. If Round 2 runs over, skip Round 3 and go to the exit ticket.

Opening words

“A basis is a coordinate system. Today we will translate the same vector and the same linear map between coordinate systems.”

把基看成坐标系统。今天练习在不同坐标系统之间翻译向量和线性映射。

Course link

Week 9 lecture focus is change-of-basis matrices and the matrix Mᴮₐ(f). Problem Sheet 8 is available, and the next hand-in is due next Wednesday.

本周重点是换基矩阵和 Mᴮₐ(f)。先把坐标变换方向说清楚。

Key conventions

  1. A={a₁,…,aₙ} means an ordered basis. Columns of Cᴱₐ are the A-basis vectors written in E-coordinates.
  2. Cᴬᴮ converts B-coordinates to A-coordinates. Composition follows the superscripts and subscripts.
  3. Mᴮₐ(f) maps A-coordinates of an input to B-coordinates of its output.
Recall · 09:00–09:05 · no notes

Three quick questions

Give students two minutes alone, then check together.

1 · Ordered basis

What does A={a₁,a₂} mean? Say what the braces record.

Board answerAn ordered basis, so coordinates depend on the order of the listed vectors.

2 · Coordinate vector

What is a coordinate vector? Use a basis B.

Board answer[v]ᴮ is the coefficient column expressing v as a linear combination of the ordered basis B.

3 · Matrix of a linear map

What does a matrix of a linear map do? Name input and output coordinates.

Board answerMᴮₐ(f)[v]ₐ = [f(v)]ᴮ.
Round 1 · 09:05–09:20 · 15 min

Change-of-basis matrices

WATCH → TRY → COMPARE · The Week 9 tutor plan’s central activity.

Worked example · read the columns

Let E={(1,0)ᵀ,(0,1)ᵀ}, A={(1,1)ᵀ,(−1,1)ᵀ}, B={(6,0)ᵀ,(0,4)ᵀ}. Find Cᴱₐ and Cᴱᵦ.

Board answerCᴱₐ=[[1,−1],[1,1]] and Cᴱᵦ=[[6,0],[0,4]]. Each column is one basis vector written in E-coordinates.

Your turn · compute Cᴬᴱ and Cᴮᴱ

Find the inverses of the two matrices above, and state what each inverse converts.

Board answerCᴬᴱ=(Cᴱₐ)⁻¹=½[[1,1],[−1,1]]. Cᴮᴱ=diag(1/6,1/4). They convert E-coordinates to A- and B-coordinates.
Say “The notation tells you the direction. The basis named downstairs is the input coordinate system. Check your answer by multiplying the two opposite changes and getting the identity.”
每列是一根基向量在目标坐标系中的坐标。换向量方向时取逆矩阵。
Fast check What is Cᴬᴬ?
Board answer · The identity matrix. Staying in the same ordered basis changes no coordinates.
Round 2 · 09:20–09:35 · 15 min

Coordinate changes and linear maps

WATCH → TRY → COMPARE · Make the composition order visible.

Worked example · compute Mᴮᴮ(f) directly

Let Mᴱᴱ(f)=[[1,−1],[1,1]], E={(1,0)ᵀ,(0,1)ᵀ}, and B={(1,0)ᵀ,(−1,1)ᵀ}. Find Mᴮᴮ(f) by applying f to the B-basis vectors and expressing the outputs in B.

Board answerf(1,0)=(1,1)=2·(1,0)+1·(−1,1), while f(−1,1)=(−2,0)=−2·(1,0)+0·(−1,1). Hence Mᴮᴮ(f)=[[2,−2],[1,0]]. Use the coordinate equations, do not guess from the standard matrix.

Your turn · use a composition

Explain the identity Mᴮₐ(f)=Cᴮᴱ Mᴱᴱ(f) Cᴱₐ. What happens to a coordinate column v?

Board answerFirst Cᴱₐ converts [v]ₐ to [v]ᴱ. Then Mᴱᴱ(f) produces [f(v)]ᴱ. Finally Cᴮᴱ converts that to [f(v)]ᴮ. Matrix multiplication is read right to left.
Say “Do not multiply until you can narrate the three stages. The product is a record of the route taken by the coordinate column.”
先用语言说三步，再写矩阵乘积。右边先作用。
Fast check When does Mᴮₐ(f) equal Cᴮₐ?
Board answer · When f is the identity map from the vector space to itself. Then the only change is the coordinate system.
Round 3 + exit · 09:35–09:55 · optional if time

Basis vectors, matrix columns and checks

Connect to Problem Sheet 8.

Worked example · columns as images

Why does the j-th column of Mᴮₐ(f) equal [f(aⱼ)]ᴮ?

Board answerThe j-th A-coordinate column is the coordinate vector of aⱼ. Applying the map to that basis input gives f(aⱼ), and the matrix column records its B-coordinates.

Your turn · matrix sanity checks

List two checks that catch a wrong change-of-basis matrix.

Board answerCheck that CᴬᴱCᴱₐ=I and that each column of Cᴱₐ is the stated A-basis vector. For a map, test one basis vector directly.

Exit ticket

  1. What does Cᴱₐ do to [v]ₐ?
  2. Which matrix acts first in Cᴮᴱ Mᴱᴱ(f) Cᴱₐ?
  3. What is the quickest way to construct Mᴮₐ(f) without a change-of-basis formula?
Answers · It gives [v]ᴱ. Cᴱₐ acts first. Apply f to each A-basis vector and write the outputs in B-coordinates as columns.
At home Complete the relevant change-of-basis questions from Problem Sheet 8 and revisit the lecture’s matrix notation. Bring one coordinate-direction question to the next tutorial.
